How Khoj Works

Designed specifically for Indian college campuses where belongings are described in conversational Hindi, English, and Hinglish.

1

Speak or Type

Describe your lost or found item naturally in your preferred language. You can speak into your microphone or type freely.

2

Sarvam AI Understood

Sarvam's 105B conversational model extracts structured attributes: item category, colors, locations, brands, and key features.

3

Explainable Matching

Weighted algorithms compare category (30%), features (25%), color/brand (15%), location (15%), and time (15%) to rank high probability matches.

4

Safe Verification

Claimants provide private verification proof before collection. Roll numbers and IDs are masked to protect student privacy.
